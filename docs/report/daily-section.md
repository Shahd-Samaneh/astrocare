## Daily health

The Daily feature turns three everyday habits into data the crew member can see and act on: sleep, exercise and a quick check on how they feel. It shows the last seven nights against the person's own average, offers a simple exercise plan, and, when exercise cannot be done because of pain, fatigue, equipment or time, suggests a lighter or equipment-free alternative. A short checklist on the Today page keeps the day's tasks in one place. NASA's human research program lists sleep loss, reduced fitness, bone changes and the effects of isolation among the risks of long missions, which is why these habits are tracked. All data is simulated, and the exercise plan is an example inspired by ISS devices (ARED, T2, CEVIS), not a prescription. Limits: thresholds are team decisions, sleep ratings are subjective, there is no wearable data, and the app only points out possible links. It never diagnoses, suggests medication, or implies NASA endorsement.
